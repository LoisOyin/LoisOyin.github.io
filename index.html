About

Energy systems with a transition mindset

I am an Energy Systems Engineer pursuing an Erasmus Mundus Master's in Decentralised Smart Energy Systems, with an academic background in Electrical & Electronics Engineering.

My work sits at the intersection of energy systems, energy markets, policy and the business realities of the energy transition. I am particularly interested in understanding how technical energy solutions translate into economic, regulatory and commercial outcomes.

My experience and academic work span battery storage and energy systems modelling, energy markets and techno-economic analysis, as well as energy policy and regulation. My current work includes research on sustainable aviation fuels, e-fuels and European energy-transition policy, while my academic projects have explored battery sizing and optimisation, PV-battery systems, electricity markets and energy-system design.

I am interested in the wider development of a cleaner and more resilient energy system, particularly where engineering, economics, policy and business come together.
