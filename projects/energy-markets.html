Energy Markets & Economic Analysis

A deep dive into how electricity markets work and how market mechanisms translate into financial outcomes.

In this project, I analysed day-ahead, intraday and balancing electricity markets, working with merit order, market clearing and balancing prices. I also explored how changes in electricity supply and demand can affect market prices and financial outcomes.

What I found particularly interesting was the link between the technical operation of the power system and the economic decisions happening around it. It gave me a practical introduction to analysing energy markets not just from a systems perspective, but also through the lens of pricing, revenues, market exposure and commercial performance.

This project strengthened my interest in the intersection of energy systems, energy markets, economics and business decision-making.
