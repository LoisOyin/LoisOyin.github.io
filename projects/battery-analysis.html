01 · BATTERY MODELLING

Battery Modelling, Sizing & Optimisation

How large does a battery actually need to be when the system around it keeps changing?

In this project, I developed a Python-based optimisation model using Pyomo and Gurobi to investigate battery sizing and operation in a residential energy system integrating PV, EV charging, a heat pump, the grid and a backup generator.

I modelled battery dynamics and operational constraints across a full year at 15-minute resolution, exploring how changes in system configuration, demand and energy prices affected storage requirements.

One comparison in particular showed just how much the role of a battery can change when the grid is no longer there to help...

02 · ENERGY SYSTEM DESIGN

Optimal Design of a Local Energy Network

What does it take to keep an entire community powered when there is no grid to fall back on?

In this project, I designed an off-grid PV-battery DC microgrid for a 50-household community in rural Nepal, focusing on the sizing and performance of LFP battery storage. Using Python and MATLAB, I modelled the system and optimised its PV and battery capacity under different operating conditions.

The project became a deeper look into battery sizing, reliability and cost, with some interesting results along the way...
