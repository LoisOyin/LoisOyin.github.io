Policy Analysis

My first real deep dive into policy, regulatory and market analysis.

I’ve always been fascinated by EU policies, especially those connected to decarbonisation, carbon emissions, and the transition to a cleaner energy system. From CBAM and CORSIA to policies shaping sustainable fuels and energy markets, I find it particularly interesting to see how policy decisions translate into real-world economic and business implications.

In this work, I explored EU regulation alongside market analysis and the economic and financial implications of policy decisions. A particular area of interest in this work was sustainable aviation fuels and the policy landscape (RefuelEU) shaping its development, giving me an opportunity to explore how regulation can influence the future of aviation and the broader transition towards lower-carbon fuels.

This is the beginning of my policy and regulatory analysis journey, and I’m looking forward to exploring much more.
