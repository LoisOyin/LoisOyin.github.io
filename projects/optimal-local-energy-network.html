Optimal Design of a Local Energy Network

What happens when you have to design an entire energy system for a community and make sure the sun, batteries and electricity demand all work together?

In this project, I explored the design of an off-grid PV-battery energy network for a rural community in Nepal. I modelled the community's energy needs and renewable generation, then used optimisation techniques to explore how the system could be sized for reliable and cost-effective operation.

The project brought together renewable energy modelling, battery storage, optimisation and techno-economic analysis, with a particular focus on understanding the trade-offs between system reliability, cost and energy utilisation.
