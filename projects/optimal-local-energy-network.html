Optimal Design of a Local Energy Network

What does it take to keep an entire community powered when there is no grid to fall back on?

In this project, I designed an off-grid PV-battery DC microgrid for a 50-household community in rural Nepal, focusing on the sizing and performance of LFP battery storage. Using Python and MATLAB, I modelled the system and optimised its PV and battery capacity under different operating conditions.

The project became a deeper look into battery sizing, reliability and cost, with some interesting results along the way...
